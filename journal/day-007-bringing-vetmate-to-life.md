## Features

Today I completed the entire Features section.

This wasn't just creating a few cards.

I built the whole layout from scratch.

Each feature has its own heading, subheading, highlighted keywords, description, and a dedicated placeholder where the real VetMate screenshots will go later.

Every feature also has its own floating badge with a smooth floating animation that matches the Hero.

I added fade-in animations as each feature enters the screen while scrolling, making the whole section feel much more interactive.

I also built the **Learn More** buttons and added hover animations to every single one of them.

One thing I'm really happy with is the auto-scrolling badge section.

The badges scroll horizontally in an infinite loop while also floating up and down, and after a lot of experimenting, I finally got the animation feeling smooth instead of distracting.

By the end of today, the Features section was basically complete.

The only thing left is replacing the placeholder images with actual screenshots of the VetMate app once it's ready.
