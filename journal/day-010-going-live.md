## What I Did Today

Today I worked on the authentication side of VetMate.

I made the **Sign In / Login page** and tried out two different layouts before deciding which one actually fit the project.

I ended up going with the older layout because it felt more minimalistic and matched the VetMate theme way better. The second one wasn't bad, but it just didn't feel like VetMate to me.

That honestly feels like a pretty big milestone.

## Sign In / Login

I experimented with two different layouts for the login page.

I don't want every page to look overloaded just because I can add more stuff to it. I want the UI to stay simple and actually feel connected to the rest of the website.
