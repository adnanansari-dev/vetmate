# Day 001 – Building the Navbar

## Date

30 July 2026

---

## What I Did Today

Started building the first actual part of the website today—the Navbar.

At first I had no idea how React components worked, but after playing around with them for a while, they finally started making sense.

I created my first Navbar component, imported it into the project and got everything showing on the screen.

I also learned how to use Flexbox to arrange everything properly. It looked pretty basic by the end of today, but at least I finally had something on the screen that I built myself.

---

## What I Learned

Today was mostly about understanding React.

I finally understood that components are basically reusable pieces of the website instead of putting everything into one huge file.

I also got a better understanding of Flexbox. It was confusing at first, but after trying it a few times, I started understanding how it controls spacing and alignment.

Tailwind is also starting to make more sense. Instead of writing CSS files, I can build most of the layout directly with utility classes.
