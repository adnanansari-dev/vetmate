## Date

09 August 2026

## What I Did Today

Today I finally started getting deeper into the actual app.

The landing page is already live, so now I'm moving on from just making VetMate look good to actually making the whole thing work.

Today was mostly about authentication and onboarding.

---

## Authentication

I finished the Sign In / Login page and started working on Google authentication.

I configured Google OAuth using the v5 handlers and started connecting it with the actual authentication flow.

This is where things are getting a lot more interesting because now I'm not just writing UI.

I'm actually starting to deal with how users will enter the app and what happens after they sign in.

---

## Onboarding

I also completed the onboarding form today.

After a user signs in, I don't want to just throw them straight into the dashboard.

I want VetMate to know what type of user they are and collect the basic information needed before they get into the actual app.

I made two different options for the users:

- Veterinarian
- Livestock Keeper

I also made separate visuals for both so it's easier to understand which one you're selecting without making the whole thing look complicated.

- animations

Now I'm dealing with:
